Using ShareWiFiQR

Use ShareWiFiQR to share Wi-Fi details for networks you own or have permission to share. You are responsible for the information you enter and for keeping it up to date.

Sharing QR codes

Anyone who has your QR code or scan link can open its guest page when the live service is available. Distribute codes to your intended guests, and keep a copy of your network details so you can share them if the service is unavailable.

Account access

Keep your portal sign-in credentials secure and use an email address you control. This prototype uses demo accounts and sample payment history.
