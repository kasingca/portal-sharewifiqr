Draft for the ShareWiFiQR portal.

Your information

We never share your information with third parties. The portal uses your first name, last name, and email address for your account profile.

Information you share with guests

The Wi-Fi details you enter are intended for anyone you give the QR code or scan link to. Anyone who receives a forwarded link can also open the guest page in the live service.

Prototype data

Changes in this demo are stored in your current browser. Use sample Wi-Fi passwords and profile details while trying it out.
